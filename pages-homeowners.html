Questions

Homeowner questions

Is this the same as composite decking?

No. Most composite decking is a blend of wood fibre and plastic. Our boards are recycled polyethylene and polypropylene with no wood fibre, so there is nothing to absorb water or rot.

How much does it cost?

Use the estimator for a materials range for your project. Our final price list is being set for the Canadian launch, so every figure on the site is an estimate until you get a quote.

Can I install it myself?

Yes, with standard woodworking tools. Follow the installation guide for joist spacing and gaps. For the best result and an easier warranty, use an installer from our directory.

Where do the products come from?

They are manufactured in Brazil by InBrasil from post-industrial plastic waste and imported and supported in Canada by us.

What is the warranty?

Warranty terms for Canada are being confirmed with the manufacturer. Each product page will show the confirmed terms, and you can register your project online.
