    Questions
    About the estimate
    
    
    
          How accurate is it?
          
    Quantities follow the installation guide and include a waste allowance. Prices are ranges until our Canadian price list is final. A quote confirms everything.
    
        
    
          Why can't I choose a wider joist spacing?
          
    Each board has a maximum span. The estimator will not let you exceed it, because boards spaced too widely can sag.
